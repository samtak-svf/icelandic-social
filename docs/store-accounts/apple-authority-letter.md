# Apple authority letter (template)

Apple support asks for this after the enrollment form is submitted. Copy the text below onto
Samtak svf.'s letterhead, fill the blanks, and have both signatories sign the PDF with a
Dokobit QES. Every blank is filled by hand from the source named in it; nothing here is
guessed, names and patronymics least of all.

The letter is in English because Apple's reviewers read English. Names may keep their
Icelandic letters in the signature block; the organisation name and address are ASCII to
match the D-U-N-S record.

---

**Samtak svf.**
[registered address, ASCII, as on the D-U-N-S record]
112 Reykjavik, Iceland

[date]

To: Apple Developer Program Support
Re: Enrollment ID [enrollment ID from the form]

To whom it may concern,

Samtak svf. intends to enroll in the Apple Developer Program as an organization.

We confirm that [applicant's full name], [applicant's title], who submitted enrollment
[enrollment ID], is authorized to act on behalf of Samtak svf., to bind Samtak svf. to the
Apple Developer Program License Agreement and other legal agreements with Apple, and to
manage the organization's developer account.

D-U-N-S number: [D-U-N-S]

Signed for Samtak svf.:

[full name], Managing Director (framkvæmdastjóri)
[email] · [phone, written in by hand on the PDF, never committed]

[full name], Chair of the Board (formaður)
[email] · [phone, written in by hand on the PDF, never committed]

---

If the applicant is also one of the two signatories, the letter still carries both
signatures: Apple asked for the delegating party's signature as well in 2026-05.
